More than martial arts. A way of life.

Taekwondo is not only about fighting. It is about developing discipline, confidence, physical fitness, respect, focus and the mindset to keep improving. The Tae-Kwon-Do Sports Association of Ahmedabad has worked since 2013 to spread the sport in Gujarat and give young athletes a capable platform.
